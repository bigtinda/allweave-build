# Provider logos

Most provider SVGs in this directory are from `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.95.1, licensed under MIT; see `LICENSE`.

`ant-ling.ico` is the favicon linked by the official Ant Ling site. `radius.ico` is the light-theme favicon linked by the official Radius site. `typesafe.png` is the favicon linked by the official TypeSafe AI site.

`kimi.svg` is the light-background K mark from the [official Moonshot AI brand guide](https://moonshotai.github.io/Branding-Guide/).

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners. The logos are used only to identify providers in the application.
